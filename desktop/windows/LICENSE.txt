1. AUTHORIZED INSTITUTIONAL USE
This software is provided for authorized digital forensic evidence
management, chain-of-custody tracking, and judicial review.

2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE INTEGRITY
FORENZA computes deterministic SHA-256 integrity hashes for all
ingested evidence media and canonical metadata. Users agree not to
attempt unauthorized modification of local encrypted vaults.

3. ISO/IEC 27037 & NIST SP 800-86 COMPLIANCE
The application adheres to recognized digital evidence handling
and forensic laboratory controls.

4. PRIVACY & LOCAL SECURITY
All local evidence is stored using AES-256 authenticated encryption
within private application directories.
