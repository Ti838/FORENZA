## 1. Compliance Mapping Table

| Standard & Clause | Standard Requirement | FORENZA Implemented Capability | Implementation Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ISO/IEC 27037:2012 §6** | Digital evidence handling: identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation. | Deterministic SHA-256 content hashing, canonical RFC 8785 manifests, Ed25519 device signatures, immutable storage. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **ISO/IEC 27038:2015** | Digital redaction: ensuring redacted documents do not expose original data while preserving integrity. | `derived_artifacts` tracking with explicit parent hash, derivation tool version, and redaction rationale. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **NIST SP 800-86** | Integrating forensic techniques into incident response. | Security Event Center (FZ-SECURITY), structured forensic audit trails, append-only custody logs. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Federal Rules of Evidence 902(14)** | Certified records generated by an electronic process with hash comparison. | Certified Court Dossier PDF with cryptographic verification manifest and master hash certificate. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **ISO/IEC 27001 / SOC 2** | Role-based access control, cryptographic key management, audit trail immutability. | RBAC + ABAC authorization, DB-level immutability triggers, TOTP MFA, device trust. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Chain of Custody Legal Best Practices** | Unbroken custody tracking from scene to courtroom. | Blockchain-style SHA-256 custody chain + Merkle state DAG with single-use nonce handover. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Physical Evidence Protocols** | Tamper-evident seals, container condition tracking. | Physical container registry, barcode seals (`INTACT`/`BROKEN`), condition photo hashes. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Institutional SOP** | Evidence retention, destruction authorizations, legal holds. | `legal_holds` and `legal_authorizations` with delete-prevention triggers. | **REQUIRES ORGANIZATIONAL SOP** |

> **DISCLAIMER:**  
> FORENZA implements technical controls aligned with the above international standards. Formal legal certification requires institutional accreditation and adherence to institutional Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
